1 Start.



2 Initialize an empty order list.



3 Display the restaurant menu.



4 Ask the customer to select a food item.



5 Check whether the selection is valid.



6 If valid, accept the quantity.



7 Add the selected food item, price and quantity to the order list.



8 Ask whether the customer wants to order another item.



9 If yes, repeat the selection process.



10 Otherwise, display all selected orders.



11 Stop.
